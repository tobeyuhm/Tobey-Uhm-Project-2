## 4. Robustness, holdout and the highest-risk claim

**Highest-risk claim:** "the constrained portfolio (85% BIL / 15% ACWI) is a mandate-compliant way to meet CPI + 4%." It rests on one estimate, ACWI's 20.8% trailing mean. Three challenges are aimed at it:

1. **Stress run** (15 scenarios on estimates, window, costs and constraints; `output/report.md`):
   - Estimates, costs and constraints barely move it (0-5 points).
   - The window moves it 10 points (24 months), 30 points (60 months) and 25 points (120 months).
   - The 60-month result trips stop test S2, because the 25% limit equals S7's annual turnover limit: an estimate change alone should not force more trading than the client allows in a year.
2. **Walk-forward holdout**, Jan 2012 - Oct 2026: 15 December decisions, each seeing only data to that date, with trading costs charged.
   - The process returned 6.33% a year against 7.68% for the benchmark and missed the 6.5% hurdle.
   - It stopped in 3 years and broke the turnover limit in 12 of 14 later rebalances.
   - Its 2022 loss of -10.8% trips stop test S3.
   - Windows of 24, 60 and 120 months do not fix it (table in the report).
3. **The fallback's own limit:** the recommended reserve range returned 7.20-7.67% but lost 15.3-15.8% in 2022. This is why the decision includes a CIO question rather than a claim that the fallback meets S9.

**Signal test:** IEF minus BIL by the prior-month curve sign, 2008-2026. Positive curve: +1.79%/yr, t = 1.07 (183 months). Inverted: +0.34%/yr, t = 0.08 (39 months). At the annual rebalances the signal never acted. Disposition: kept as a pre-registered monitored rule, with no weight in the decision.

**How the limitations change the action:** the S2 and S3 trips and the underperformance are the reason for REJECT. The S9 measurement weakness is the reason for committee ask 3. The sample size (15 decisions) is the reason the process stays on a paper track with explicit re-open triggers instead of being discarded.

## 8. Limitations

- Historical means drive the optimizer. That instability is the finding.
- The S9 measure (95% parametric loss) understated 2022 for every strategy. A CIO-confirmed measure is the precondition for any pilot.
- CPI is fixed at 2.5% in the realised test.
- The grid step is 5 points.
- The walk-forward has 15 annual decisions.
- Taxes are ignored.
- Trading costs are assumed.
- Monthly data cannot show intra-month liquidity stress (M4 is non-binding for daily-traded ETFs).
